Morning Fog

By The Unsaid Page • Featured Poem

The world is soft today,
Edges blurred by the grey breath
Of a sleeping giant.

The trees have lost their footing,
Floating rootless in the white,
Like memories half-forgotten
In the quiet light.

I reach out my hand,
To catch the ghost of rain,
But grasp only air,
And the silence of the lane.
